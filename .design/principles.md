# PDOS: Design Principles

PlotArmour and its holdings are built as a premium editorial technology ecosystem. We reject standard corporate SaaS landing pages.

## Core Pillars

1. **Editorial Quality**
   - Think like a magazine or journal publisher (e.g. *The New York Times*, *Monocle*), not a software marketing page.
   - High information density combined with absolute legibility.

2. **Software Precision**
   - Balance classic layout structures with developer-level alignment details (inspired by *Resend* and *Linear*).
   - Clean hairline borders, structured metadata indices, and monospace system coordinates.

3. **Restraint & Calmness**
   - A calm, monochrome aesthetic. Hierarchy is created by typography contrast, white space, and editorial photography rather than color noise or animations.
