# PDOS: Anti-Patterns (What to Avoid)

These rules prevent the design system from drifting back to standard corporate layouts.

## Anti-Patterns

1. **Don't Use Uniform Card Grids Everywhere**
   - Grid cards should not repeat block after block. Alternate cards with full-width images, text blocks, and timelines.

2. **Don't Remove Pages Without Migration**
   - The ecosystem must feel complete. Never delete routes; always provide clean fallback pages or permanent redirects.

3. **Don't Introduce Color Gradients or Glows**
   - Colors should be pure monochrome. Use accent dots or badges *only* where explicitly needed for individual company branding.

4. **Don't Compress Spacing to Save Height**
   - We do not design above-the-fold landing page shortcuts. Let the visitor scroll through generous whitespace (140-200px gaps).

5. **Don't Invent New UI Components**
   - Use the approved mastheads, simple buttons, hairline blocks, and cards. Do not add custom dashboard panels or neon interactive targets.
