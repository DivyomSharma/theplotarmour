# PDOS: Imagery Guidelines

A venture studio/holding website must show visual weight. We use high-contrast monochrome photography to illustrate active domains.

## Image Properties

- **Grayscale**: All photography must be strictly monochrome.
- **Contrast**: High contrast with deep blacks and clean whites.
- **Subject matter**: Professional whiteboards, wireframe sketches, modern manufacturing components, blueprints, clean desks, notebooks, and architectural concrete layouts.
- **Aspect Ratio**: Prefer `16:10` for cards and `21:9` for wide editorial blocks.
